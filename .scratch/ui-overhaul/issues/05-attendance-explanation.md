## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** `GET /class-meetings/:id/attendance` returns the attendance policy thresholds the records were computed under, and the class length used as the denominator.

**Current behavior:**
- `meetingAttendanceSchema` returns the state, finalization and lock times, and the records.
- The meeting's ATTENDANCE policy is snapshotted on the meeting (`policySnapshot`), and each record stores `effectiveSeconds` (the denominator used).
- The roll-call page says only "Tap a student to change their status; a reason is required."

**Desired behavior:**
- The response adds `rule: { basis, presentThresholdBp, partialThresholdBp, lateGraceMinutes }` from the meeting's policy snapshot (the policy's defaults when there is no snapshot), and `ranSeconds: number | null` (the largest `effectiveSeconds` among the records, or null with no records).
- The page shows "class ran N min" when `ranSeconds` is known. It explains: "Present means in class for at least {present}% of the time it ran; Partial, at least {partial}%." The percentages come from basis points. For the SCHEDULED basis it says "of the scheduled time" instead of "of the time it ran".
- Nothing is hard-coded. A different policy snapshot gives different words.

**Key interfaces:**
- `meetingAttendanceSchema.rule` and `meetingAttendanceSchema.ranSeconds`

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The integration test shows the snapshot's thresholds (a non-default policy proves they aren't hard-coded), and `ranSeconds` after a computed class.
- [ ] A component or unit test covers the explanation text for ACTUAL and SCHEDULED, and a whole-number percentage from basis points (7500 → 75%, 6667 → 66.67%).
- [ ] Typecheck, lint, unit, integration and budget checks pass.

**Out of scope:**
- Changing how attendance is computed.
