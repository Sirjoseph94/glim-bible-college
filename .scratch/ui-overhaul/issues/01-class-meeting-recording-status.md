# 01: Recording status on Class Meetings (API change)

**What to build:** Class Meeting responses say whether the meeting's recording is expected, processing or ready to watch. The Class Meeting card (`docs/ui-design-handoff.md` §9.14) can then show "Recording processing" and "Recording ready" with a "Watch recording" link, instead of leaving an ended meeting with no next step.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Category:** enhancement

**Why:** Decision UI-Q3 (`docs/decisions.md`, 10 Oct 2026). The handoff's display states `processing` and `available` have no source in today's API. `classMeetingSchema` (`packages/contracts/src/api/meetings.ts`) returns `state` (`SCHEDULED, OPEN, LIVE, ENDED, CANCELLED, NO_SHOW`) and `recordingMode`, but nothing about the recording. The link already exists in the database: `videos.class_meeting_id` (`packages/db/src/schema/video.ts`).

## Proposed contract

Add one nullable field to `classMeetingSchema`, returned by `GET /me/schedule` and the offering's Class Meeting list:

```ts
recording: z
  .object({
    videoId: z.uuid(),
    status: z.enum(["PROCESSING", "PUBLISHED"]),
  })
  .nullable(),
```

- `PUBLISHED` only while the video is shown to students (INV-9: available and embeddable). `videoId` is then the `/recordings/$videoId` target.
- Students see `PROCESSING` or `null` for anything else (`UNPUBLISHED`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `FAILED`, `DELETED`), so nothing unplayable leaks. Staff who manage the meeting may need the full `videos.status`; decide during triage.
- `null` when no video is linked. The UI then shows "Recording expected" for an `ENDED` meeting with `recordingMode` ≠ `OFF`.

## Display mapping (UI side, after this lands)

| API | Card state | Word |
|---|---|---|
| `state = ENDED`, `recording = null`, `recordingMode ≠ OFF` | ended | Ended · "Recording expected" |
| `recording.status = PROCESSING` | processing | Recording processing |
| `recording.status = PUBLISHED` | available | Recording ready + `secondary` "Watch recording" |

- [ ] Authorization: a student sees `recording` only for meetings of offerings they're enrolled in (same rule as the schedule itself).
- [ ] Tests, including the deny path: an unpublished or failed video appears as `null`/`PROCESSING` to a student, never as `PUBLISHED`.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Class Meetings carry the status of their recording, and the Class Meeting card shows "Recording processing", "Recording ready" (with "Watch recording"), and for staff "Ready to publish" or "Recording failed".

**Current behavior:**
A Class Meeting as the API returns it (`classMeetingSchema`, used by the student schedule, the offering's Class Meeting list and every create/update/cancel response) has `state` and `recordingMode`, but nothing about the recording. The card shows "Recording expected" for an `ENDED` meeting whose `recordingMode` isn't `OFF`, and then nothing more, even after a video is linked to the meeting.

Videos link to a meeting through `videos.class_meeting_id`, and there can be several per meeting (a pasted YouTube link and a Meet upload, or more than one Meet recording). A Meet recording gets its video row as soon as it's found, with status `PROCESSING`, in the same transaction. A failed upload sets the video to `FAILED`; a retry puts it back to `PROCESSING`. So the video's status alone is enough, and the `recordings` table isn't needed.

**Desired behavior:**
Every `ClassMeeting` returned has a `recording` field: either `null` or `{ videoId, status }`. What it says depends on the viewer.

*Students, and anyone else who doesn't manage the offering:*
- `PUBLISHED` only when that student could play the video now. The rule is the same one the student's video list and playback already use: video status `PUBLISHED`, `visibility = ENROLLED`, and `publish_after` empty or in the past. `videoId` is the target of `/recordings/$videoId`.
- `PROCESSING` when a linked video is processing.
- Otherwise `null`. This covers videos that are unpublished, unavailable, failed or deleted, and **published videos the student can't watch** (staff-only, or not yet released). Nothing should hint that a hidden video exists.

*Staff who can manage the offering's videos* (the same permission check the video admin uses, decided per offering, because one schedule can mix offerings the viewer teaches with ones they're enrolled in):
- `PUBLISHED` when the video is published (they can play it whatever its visibility or release time).
- `NOT_PUBLISHED` when the video status is `UNPUBLISHED`.
- `PROCESSING` when it's processing.
- `FAILED` when the video status is `FAILED` or `UNAVAILABLE` (YouTube took it down).
- Otherwise `null` (deleted).

*More than one video:* pick the most useful one the viewer may see, newest first within each status. Students: `PUBLISHED` before `PROCESSING`. Staff: `PUBLISHED`, then `NOT_PUBLISHED`, then `PROCESSING`, then `FAILED`.

*Card (the student and Lecturer views of the Class Meeting card):*

| Meeting | Card says |
|---|---|
| `ENDED`, `recording = null`, `recordingMode ≠ OFF` | "Recording expected" (unchanged) |
| `recording.status = PROCESSING` | "Recording processing" |
| `PUBLISHED` | "Recording ready" plus a secondary "Watch recording" link to `/recordings/$videoId` |
| `NOT_PUBLISHED` (staff only) | "Ready to publish" |
| `FAILED` (staff only) | "Recording failed" |

The words follow the existing card's status style and the design tokens, which lint enforces.

**Key interfaces:**
- `classMeetingSchema` gains `recording: { videoId: uuid, status: "PROCESSING" | "PUBLISHED" | "NOT_PUBLISHED" | "FAILED" } | null`. Contract change first. The `ClassMeeting` type follows.
- One shared place builds the Class Meeting view and fills `recording`, given the viewer, so every endpoint that returns a `ClassMeeting` agrees. Lists fetch the recordings for all their meetings in one query (no N+1).
- The student rule must reuse, or exactly match, the "what a student may see" filter the video list and playback use, so the two can't drift.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `GET /me/schedule` and the offering's Class Meeting list return `recording` for each meeting. A list item stays ≤ 2 KB.
- [ ] Student deny paths (integration tests): a video that is unpublished, failed, unavailable, deleted, staff-only, or published with a future `publish_after` gives `recording: null` for the student, never `PUBLISHED`.
- [ ] A student only gets `recording` for meetings of offerings they're enrolled in (the existing schedule rule; no new leak).
- [ ] A Lecturer of the offering sees `NOT_PUBLISHED` for an unpublished video and `FAILED` for a failed or unavailable one. The same Lecturer viewing their schedule sees the student mapping for an offering they're only enrolled in.
- [ ] With a pasted published link and a processing Meet upload on one meeting, the student sees `PUBLISHED` with the link's `videoId`.
- [ ] A Meet recording found by the fake provider makes the meeting show `PROCESSING` straight away, and `PUBLISHED` once processing finishes and the video is published.
- [ ] The card shows each state in the table above, covered by component tests. "Watch recording" goes to `/recordings/$videoId`.
- [ ] typecheck, lint, deps:check, unit, integration and budget checks pass; the e2e suite has no new failures.

**Out of scope:**
- A "Continue watching" card, watch progress, or data estimates (UI #02).
- Publishing, retrying or deleting from the card. Staff keep using the video admin and "Upload again" there.
- Any call to YouTube or Google (the join path and lists stay DB-only).
- New notifications for "recording ready".
